Using the planner

Read a night

Select a night to see its twilight, Moon events, and usable windows. Friday’s night includes Saturday morning.

DayCivilNauticalAstronomical twilightAstronomical darkness
  • Black marks astronomical darkness while the Moon is below the horizon. A gray wash marks moonlight during astronomical darkness.
  • Accepted moonlight has a green tint in Light and Dark, and a dashed outline in Observing.
  • Green duration bars show how much time your settings count as usable. Check the individual windows if you need an uninterrupted session.

The four views

River

Year shows seasonal patterns. In Month, time runs left to right and each row opens for details.

Lunations

Compare nights aligned around new moon. Time runs from evening at the top to morning at the bottom. Use Full cycle for all nights or ±N for a closer look. Totals and weekend picks cover the nights shown.

Calendar

Month shows each date’s hours and windows. Week puts seven nights on one clock. Year lets you jump to a month.

Dark Weekends

Compare the best Fri–Sat and Thu–Sun trips for each new moon in the loaded range. Expand a cycle to see what Thursday and Sunday add to the same weekend. Open a weekend for its nightly windows. Use the chart buttons to open the first night of a trip. Sunday night includes Monday morning.

Planning settings

  • For moon-free usable dark, keep the Moon threshold at 0° and crescent ignoring off.
  • Raise the Moon height threshold to accept time while the Moon is low.
  • The crescent setting accepts all astronomical darkness when the Moon’s illumination at local midnight is below your chosen percentage.
  • New-moon focus highlights nights near new moon. The usable windows for each night stay the same.

See how Atlas calculates usable windows.

Sites and time

Use Manage sites to add decimal coordinates or paste a full Google Maps link with coordinates. Check the suggested time zone, especially near a boundary. Elevation is optional.

Times use the observing site’s clock, including daylight saving. The header clock shows the current time there as you browse future nights.

Sharing and browser data

  • More → Share site makes an invitation that opens your location around today. A new site is saved automatically in the recipient’s browser, with Undo. Existing sites are reused.
  • More → Share plan links to particular dates, view, and Moon settings. Those settings become the recipient’s current choices. Choose Include this site if they should use your location. A new site has a Save site offer they can dismiss while continuing to use the plan.
  • Manage sites → Browser data exports or imports sites and preferences for backups or moving between browsers.
