Using the planner

On phones, open Plan settings to change the date range, new-moon focus or Moon thresholds. Changes apply immediately; choose Done to return to the chart. Site, view tabs, Tonight and the labeled live countdown stay in the header in either orientation. Tap or keyboard-activate the countdown to open Live details with the site's clock, time zone and usable-dark status. On touch screens, tap a night for its details; the desktop hover strip is omitted.

Read a night

Select a night to see its twilight, Moon events, and usable windows. Friday’s night includes Saturday morning.

  • Black marks astronomical darkness while the Moon is below the horizon. A gray wash marks moonlight during astronomical darkness. Its brightness varies with the Moon’s phase and altitude; your Moon settings determine usable time.
  • Accepted moonlight has a green tint in Light and Dark, and a dashed outline in Observing.
  • Green duration bars show how much time your settings count as usable. Check the individual windows if you need an uninterrupted session.

The four views

Open the dates under Range to choose Around now or a Calendar year. Around now shows 14 months starting with the previous month at the site. For January through December, enter a year and choose Show year. The arrows move the range by a year. Tonight jumps to the site’s current observing night and restores Around now if that night is outside the range.

River

Year shows seasonal patterns across the selected range. In Month, time runs left to right and each row opens for details.

Lunations

Compare nights aligned around new moon. Time runs from evening at the top to morning at the bottom. Use Full cycle for all nights or ±N for a closer look. Totals and weekend picks cover the nights shown.

Calendar

Month shows each date’s hours and windows. Week puts seven nights on one clock. Year shows the months in the selected range. Choose a month to open it.

Dark Weekends

Compare the best Fri–Sat and Thu–Sun trips for each new moon in the loaded range. Expand a cycle to see what Thursday and Sunday add to the same weekend. Open a weekend for its nightly windows. Use the chart buttons to open the first night of a trip. Sunday night includes Monday morning.

Planning settings

  • For moon-free usable dark, keep the Moon threshold at 0° and crescent ignoring off.
  • Raise the Moon height threshold to accept time while the Moon is low.
  • The crescent setting accepts all astronomical darkness when the Moon’s illumination at local midnight is below your chosen percentage.
  • New-moon focus highlights nights near new moon. The usable windows for each night stay the same.

Sites and time

Choose Manage sites… at the bottom of the site picker to see your saved sites. Use the up/down buttons to reorder them; each move saves immediately and the picker follows the same order. Edit opens a separate form. Save changes returns to the list; Save & use site also selects it for planning. Saving edits to the site you are currently using updates its open plan.

Add site accepts decimal coordinates or a full Google Maps link with coordinates. Check the suggested time zone, especially near a boundary. Elevation is optional. New sites go first. Each row has a pencil to edit, overlapping squares to duplicate (placed after its source), and a trash can to remove. Use selects a site; a checkmark marks the one currently in use. Removing a site leaves the current plan open. Cancel edit returns to the list, and Done closes the manager. Leaving a changed draft asks you to confirm discarding it; moves already saved remain saved.

Times use the observing site’s clock, including daylight saving. The header clock shows the current time there as you browse past or future nights. The countdown always follows now at that site and highlights the next solar transition or usable-dark start/end, whichever happens first. A later event stays secondary on desktop and remains available in Live details on phones. It shows minutes for longer waits and minutes and seconds in the final five minutes. Those seconds count toward a calculated event, not a guarantee of second-level accuracy. On phones, activate the countdown to open Live details for the clock and full status.

Sharing and browser data

  • More → Share site makes an invitation that opens your location in Around now with the recipient’s settings. It reuses a saved site in its existing position or saves a new one at the top of their list, with Undo.
  • More → Share plan links to the selected range, view, and Moon settings. Those settings become the recipient’s current choices. Choose Include this site if they should use your location. A new site has a Save site offer, which saves it at the top of the list. Recipients can dismiss the offer and continue using the plan.
  • More → Browser data → Backup and restore exports or imports all sites, their order, the saved selection and preferences. Import previews a replacement of the entire saved document; it does not add sites to your existing list. Export includes temporary changes when saving is unavailable, but never an open edit draft.

Recovery copy offers one local copy kept before import, preference reset or a saved-data format upgrade. Ordinary edits and moves do not create history. A later qualifying operation replaces the copy, and clearing browser data removes it. Download a copy to keep it outside the browser; use Import sites and preferences to review and restore it.

Maintenance clears rebuildable calculations, resets preferences while keeping sites and order, or appends missing starter sites while keeping your existing edits.
