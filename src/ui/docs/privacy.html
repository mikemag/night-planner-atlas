Privacy

Atlas has no accounts, analytics, trackers, or cookies.

Your sites and data

  • In your browser. Atlas keeps saved sites, preferences, cached calculations, and offline app files in this browser. Calculations and time-zone lookups run on your device.
  • Clearing your data. To erase Atlas data stored in this browser, close your Atlas tabs and clear this site’s data in your browser settings. To clear just the calculations, choose Manage sites → Browser data → Clear computed cache.
  • In links and downloads. Backups contain your saved sites. Shared plan links and links in calendar exports include the site name, coordinates, time zone, and any elevation only if you choose to include the site. Anyone with a copy can read those details.
  • When you load Atlas online. Firebase Hosting serves the app over HTTPS and may retain your IP address for a few months. Firebase privacy and security.
