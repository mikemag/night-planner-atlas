Privacy

Atlas has no accounts, analytics, trackers, or cookies.

Your sites and data

  • In your browser. Atlas keeps saved sites, preferences, cached calculations, saved site order, a single recovery copy when available, and offline app files in this browser. Calculations and time-zone lookups run on your device.
  • Clearing your data. To erase Atlas data stored in this browser, close your Atlas tabs and clear this site’s data in your browser settings. To clear just the calculations, choose More → Browser data → Maintenance → Clear computed cache.
  • In links and downloads. Exported backups and recovery downloads contain site details and preferences. A normal export includes the current list and order, including temporary changes if saving is unavailable, but excludes an open edit draft. Site invitations also contain site details. Plan links and links in calendar exports include the site name, coordinates, time zone, and any elevation only if you choose to include the site. Anyone with a copy can read those details. Opening a site invitation saves a new site at the top of that browser’s list, with Undo. Existing saved sites keep their position and metadata. Importing a backup replaces all sites and preferences only after review and explicit confirmation. The recovery copy is local to this browser and is removed when browser data is cleared; download it to keep an external copy.
  • When you load Atlas online. Firebase Hosting serves the app over HTTPS and may retain your IP address for a few months. Firebase privacy and security.
