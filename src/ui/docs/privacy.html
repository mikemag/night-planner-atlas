Privacy

Atlas has no accounts, analytics, trackers, or cookies.

Your sites and data

  • In your browser. Your sites, preferences, recovery copy, cached calculations, and offline app files stay in this browser. Calculations and time-zone lookups run on your device. Atlas does not upload your sites or settings.
  • Clearing your data. Close all Atlas tabs and clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove its saved data and offline files. Export anything you want to keep first using Three-dot menu (⋯) → Browser data. Downloaded and shared copies are separate.
  • Links and downloads. Backups and site invitations contain site details. Plan links and links in calendar exports include those details only when you choose to include the site. Anyone with a copy can read them.
  • Hosting. Firebase Hosting serves the app and may retain your IP address for a few months. Firebase privacy and security.
