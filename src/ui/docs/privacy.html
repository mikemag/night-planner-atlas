Privacy and credits

Your observing sites stay in your browser. Sharing them is a deliberate choice.

Your sites and data

  • In your browser. Sites and preferences live in local storage. IndexedDB caches calculations for saved sites, including their coordinates. Clearing browser site data removes both.
  • In links and backups. Backups contain your saved sites. A link includes the site name, coordinates, zone, and optional elevation when you choose Include this site. Anyone with that link can read them.
  • On the network. Atlas loads static files from its host, which receives ordinary connection metadata. Site calculations and time-zone lookups run locally. Atlas has no accounts or analytics.

How to share a plan or back up your sites.

Sun and Moon positions: Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 by Don Cross, MIT license.

Time-zone lookup: tzf-wasm 2.0.1 and tzf-rs, MIT. Boundaries: OpenStreetMap contributors, via timezone-boundary-builder and tzf-dist, data 2026e, ODbL 1.0. Download the matching boundary dataset (TZB, 4 MB).

Third-party software and data retain their own licenses. Private favorites and astronomical results are separate from the boundary dataset.

UI components: Bootstrap 5.3.8 by the Bootstrap Authors and Popper 2.11.8 by Federico Zivolo, both MIT. Their code and assets are bundled with the planner and work offline.
