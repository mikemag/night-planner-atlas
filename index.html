Get coordinates from Google Maps

If a Maps link doesn’t work, open it in Google Maps and find your intended location. Copy the point’s coordinates, then paste them in the field above:

  • Computer: right-click the point, then click the latitude/longitude at the top of the menu to copy it.
  • Phone: touch and hold an unlabeled point to drop a pin. On Android, copy the coordinates from the search box; on iPhone, find them in the pin’s place details.

Full search links containing query=latitude,longitude or q=latitude,longitude also work. A short link or the center of a map does not give us a reliable point. Pasted links are read locally and never opened by the planner.
