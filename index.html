River

—
Day Daylight, before sunset or after sunrise.
Civil Between sunset and civil dusk, or civil dawn and sunrise.
Nautical Between the civil and nautical twilight boundaries.
Astronomical twilight Between the nautical and astronomical twilight boundaries.
Astronomical darkness The Sun’s center is more than 18° below the horizon. Moonlight can brighten this interval.
Moonlight The Moon’s upper edge is above the natural horizon during astronomical darkness. The wash varies with phase and altitude, using fixed reference conditions; it is not a local sky-brightness forecast.
Accepted moonlight Moonlit time within astronomical darkness that meets your Moon settings. Brightness follows the same phase-and-altitude wash; Observing adds a dashed boundary.
Usable dark Duration bars and window brackets show the part of astronomical darkness that meets your Moon settings. By default, the Moon must be below the horizon.
Legend

    Get coordinates from Google Maps

    If a Maps link doesn’t work, open it in Google Maps and find your intended location. Copy the point’s coordinates, then paste them in the field above:

    • Computer: right-click the point, then click the latitude/longitude at the top of the menu to copy it.
    • Phone: touch and hold an unlabeled point to drop a pin. On Android, copy the coordinates from the search box; on iPhone, find them in the pin’s place details.

    Full search links containing query=latitude,longitude or q=latitude,longitude also work. A short link or the center of a map does not give us a reliable point. Pasted links are read locally and never opened by the planner.

    Blank uses sea level. A level horizon is assumed; no terrain lookup.

    Browser data

    Backup and restore

    Download all your sites, their order and preferences to keep a backup or move to another browser. The file includes your site coordinates; share it deliberately. Calculated nights are not included.

    Import replaces all saved sites, their order, the saved selection and preferences. Review the file before confirming replacement.

    Recovery copy

    Atlas keeps one local copy before importing saved data, resetting preferences or upgrading the saved-data format. The next such operation replaces that copy. Ordinary site edits and moves do not create a history. Clearing browser data removes the copy too.

    To restore a downloaded copy, use Import sites and preferences above and review the replacement.

    Maintenance

    Checking saved calculations…

    Clearing calculations keeps sites, preferences and the recovery copy. Reset preferences restores the view, planning controls and theme while keeping sites and their order. Restoring starter sites appends missing examples and keeps existing edits.
