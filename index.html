Copying coordinates and supported Maps links

In desktop Google Maps, right-click the intended point, then click its coordinate line to copy it. Full Maps search links with query=latitude,longitude or q=latitude,longitude work too. Short links and map-camera positions need clearer input. Pasted links are read locally and never opened by the planner.
